Cybersecurity Threat Intelligence & Trend Analytics

Tool: Power BI

Overview

A two-page interactive dashboard built to analyze cybersecurity incidents from 2015 to 2024, using KPI cards, slicers and visualizations. It compares industry exposure, attack sources, financial losses and resolution times.

Objective

To analyze cybersecurity incidents from 2015 to 2024 and identify trends across years, attack types, industries, attack sources, financial losses and resolution times.

Tools used

  • Power BI
  • KPI Cards
  • Slicers
  • Charts

Key findings

  • 2017 was the peak year for incidents, and 2019 was the lowest year.
  • DDoS (531) and Phishing (529) were the most frequent attack types.
  • Together, DDoS and Phishing accounted for approximately 35% of incidents.
